# Kitch chat merger: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

Kitch chat merger ("the extension") is a browser extension that shows chat messages from a streamer's channel on the other platform (Twitch or Kick) alongside the native chat.

## Data we collect

**None.** The extension does not collect, store on any server, sell, share or transfer any personal or usage data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no servers operated by the developer.

## Data stored in your browser

The extension saves the following only in your own browser, using Chrome's extension storage:

- **Settings:** your list of Twitch/Kick streamer pairs, the display mode, and the on/off switches for the extension and for automatic pairing. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome syncs these settings across your own browsers.
- **Cache:** Kick channel and chat room IDs, the result of the automatic-pairing check for each channel, and the position of the floating chat panel.

You can delete all of this at any time by removing the extension.

## Connections to third parties

To work, the extension connects only to Twitch and Kick, and only to read public information:

- **Twitch** (twitch.tv): reads public chat anonymously, without logging in, and checks whether a channel is verified (Partner).
- **Kick** (kick.com): reads public channel information (channel ID, chat room ID, verification status) and public chat.

These requests include the streamer names from your pair list and the channel you're watching. They don't include any information about you. Your use of Twitch and Kick is subject to their own privacy policies.

The extension doesn't read your Twitch or Kick account, login, cookies, private messages or any other personal information. It only runs on twitch.tv and kick.com.
